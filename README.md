# Superstore Sales Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes Superstore sales data to identify sales trends, profitability patterns, customer performance, product performance, and regional business opportunities.

The goal is to use data analysis to generate actionable business insights and support better decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab
- GitHub

## 📁 Dataset

The dataset contains sales transactions with information about:

- Orders and order dates
- Customers
- Products
- Categories and sub-categories
- Sales
- Quantity
- Discounts
- Profit
- Regions and locations

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

- Data cleaning and validation
- Category-level sales and profit analysis
- Sub-category profitability analysis
- Discount impact analysis
- Product-level loss analysis
- Monthly sales trend analysis
- Yearly sales performance
- Customer profitability analysis
- Regional performance analysis
- Top product analysis

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### Category Performance

Technology generated the highest sales and profit among the three major categories.

### Sub-Category Profitability

Tables were identified as a major loss-making sub-category, generating approximately $207K in sales but a loss of approximately $17.7K.

### Discount Impact

Higher discount levels were associated with substantially lower profit margins in the dataset. At discounts of 30% or higher, several discount levels produced negative overall profit.

### Customer Profitability

Customer revenue does not always translate into profitability. Some customers generated significant sales while producing negative profit.

### Regional Performance

The West region achieved the highest sales and profit, while the Central region had the lowest profit margin among the four regions.
